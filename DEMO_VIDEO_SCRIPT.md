# Magic Jacky Commerce: 4-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00-0:25 | Opening
"This is Magic Jacky, a cross-border commerce and FinTech case study built around Sentosa Glow Night Run. The site connects marketplace sales, live commerce, software, FX planning, logistics, and TikTok creator operations."

Action: Open https://magic-jacky-commerce.onrender.com and show the home hero.

## 0:25-0:50 | Privacy and Consent
"Before browsing, the site provides a GDPR-style cookie and privacy choice. Users can accept all cookies or continue with essential-only preferences, and the choice is remembered on the device."

Action: Show the Cookie banner and click either `Accept All` or `Cookie Settings / Decline`.

## 0:50-1:25 | Marketplace and Checkout Security
"In the Shop, customers can filter night-run equipment, add products to the bag, and prepare for a cross-border checkout demonstration. The security panel communicates the intended SSL, PCI-aware, AML, and fraud-control design. In this coursework prototype, it is not a payment processor or a certified PCI-DSS service."

Action: Open Shop, select a filter, add one product, open the bag, and point to the trust badges.

## 1:25-1:55 | Runner and Customer Journey
"The My Run area connects an event purchase to collection instructions, a pickup code, route timing, and race-night FAQs. This illustrates how commerce data can support the customer after payment."

Action: Open My Run and point to the event guidance and FAQ.

## 1:55-2:30 | Cross-border, FX, and Operations
"The Cross-border page demonstrates delivery, duty, and settlement planning. Its rates and estimates are explicitly demo planning data, so final quotes and bank terms must be confirmed with a provider. It also links directly to the TikTok Live solution and custom FinTech and software delivery."

Action: Open Cross-border, use the estimator, then point to the two CTA links.

## 2:30-3:05 | TikTok and Software
"The TikTok page lets a user choose a creator-led live kit and carry it into the Shop. The Software page explains the product, payment, CRM, warehouse, and analytics layer behind the commercial experience."

Action: Open TikTok, choose a kit, then briefly open Software.

## 3:05-3:35 | Jacky AI
"Jacky AI is available throughout the child pages. It supports event products, FX settlement, AML safeguards, TikTok live operations, software, and fulfilment. If an external AI provider is unavailable, the system continues with topic-aware fallback responses."

Action: Open `Ask Jacky AI` and ask: "How do AML controls and FX settlement work for a TikTok live launch?"

## 3:35-4:00 | Feedback and Close
"Finally, the feedback form captures a name, email, inquiry type, message, and a one-to-five rating. The demo confirms the request immediately, representing the handoff to a CRM workflow. The live deployment is available at magic-jacky-commerce.onrender.com."

Action: Open `Feedback`, submit a short form, and return to the public URL.
