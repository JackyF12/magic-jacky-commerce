# Magic Jacky Commerce: 4-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00-0:25 | Opening
"This is Magic Jacky, a cross-border commerce and FinTech case study built around Sentosa Glow Night Run. The site connects marketplace sales, live commerce, software, FX planning, logistics, and TikTok creator operations."

Action: Open https://magic-jacky-commerce.onrender.com and show the home hero.

## 0:25-0:50 | Privacy and Consent
"Before browsing, the site provides a GDPR-style cookie and privacy choice. Users can accept all cookies or continue with essential-only preferences, and the choice is remembered on the device."

Action: Show the Cookie banner and click either `Accept All` or `Cookie Settings / Decline`.

## 0:50-1:25 | Marketplace and Checkout Security
"In the Shop, customers can filter night-run equipment, add products to the bag, select a delivery market, and preview local currency, landed cost, and arrival window. The security panel communicates the intended SSL, PCI-aware, AML, and fraud-control design. In this coursework prototype, it is not a payment processor or a certified PCI-DSS service."

Action: Open Shop, select a filter, add one product, open the bag, switch the delivery market to Japan, and point to the trust controls.

## 1:25-1:55 | Live Commerce and Customer Journey
"The Live Commerce Demo turns the page into a working live-selling flow. We start the room, select the market and host language, pin an item, reserve its demo stock, collect a viewer question, and carry the live bag into the Shop."

Action: Open Live, start the demo, pin a product, add it to the live bag, send one host question, then open the live bag in Shop.

## 1:55-2:15 | Runner and Customer Journey
"The My Run area connects an event purchase to collection instructions, a pickup code, route timing, and race-night FAQs. This illustrates how commerce data can support the customer after payment."

Action: Open My Run and point to the event guidance and FAQ.

## 2:15-2:45 | Cross-border, FX, and Operations
"The Cross-border page demonstrates delivery, duty, and settlement planning. The Order Control Tower generates a saved PAY, SHIP, and SERVE fulfilment plan. Its rates and estimates are demo planning data, so final quotes and bank terms must be confirmed with a provider."

Action: Open Cross-border, generate an Order Control Tower plan, then point to the two CTA links.

## 2:45-3:15 | TikTok and Software
"The TikTok page lets a user choose a creator profile, market, and live kit to generate a campaign brief. The Software page has an Integration Builder that turns selected systems into an implementation scope."

Action: Open TikTok, generate a campaign brief, then open Software and generate a build scope.

## 3:15-3:40 | Jacky AI
"Jacky AI is available throughout the child pages. It supports event products, FX settlement, AML safeguards, TikTok live operations, software, and fulfilment. If an external AI provider is unavailable, the system continues with topic-aware fallback responses."

Action: Open `Ask Jacky AI` and ask: "How do AML controls and FX settlement work for a TikTok live launch?"

## 3:40-4:00 | Feedback and Close
"Finally, the feedback form captures a name, email, inquiry type, message, and a one-to-five rating. The demo confirms the request immediately. The live deployment is available at magic-jacky-commerce.onrender.com."

Action: Open `Feedback`, submit a short form, and return to the public URL.
